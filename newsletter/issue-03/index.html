01  /  Lead Article Standing
Three Minutes Standing Still
Standing practice · At least three minutes a day
Set a timer for three minutes. Stand. That is the whole assignment.
Feet under the hips. Soft knees. Spine tall like someone is lifting the crown of your head. Hands rest wherever they stop without fidgeting. Breathe through the nose. Eyes open or soft. No music. No checking the phone. When the timer ends, you are done.
This is standing qigong in its simplest form: hold a quiet shape long enough that your legs start telling the truth. Quads burn. Low back argues. Mind invents errands. Stay. Three minutes is short enough that you will actually do it, and long enough that something in the body settles.
How to start today
1. Phone timer, three minutes.
2. Same spot every day if you can: kitchen tile, bedroom rug, mat at the school before class.
3. If you sway, correct once and stay. If you shake, stay. If you quit early, reset and finish the three.
4. Longer later is fine. First win the three.
You do not need a perfect posture lecture to begin. You need a clock and a decision. Stand still for three minutes a day this week. Bring what you notice to the floor.
